🎬 Vedora

Download. Save. Enjoy.

Vedora is a modern Android video and audio downloader powered by "yt-dlp" (https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp).

Download videos and audio from thousands of supported websites directly to your Android device. Enjoy a simple interface, convenient download options, and offline access to your favorite media.

- 🎥 Download videos from supported websites.
- 🎵 Download audio from supported websites.
- 🌐 Powered by yt-dlp.
- ⚡ Fast and efficient downloads.
- 🎚️ Available video and audio quality options.
- 📂 Manage downloaded media.
- 📱 Designed exclusively for Android.
- 🎨 Simple and user-friendly interface.
- ▶️ Enjoy your media offline.
